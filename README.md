**"Let Him Cook"** 
is a mobile application designed as a digital platform for managing and sharing cooking recipes. The app combines personal recipe storage with a culinary community, allowing users to save their favorite recipes and discover inspiration for new dishes.
The application features a user-friendly interface, making it easy for users to find recipes based on available ingredients, cooking time, or desired cuisine types

Application Objectives:
1. Simplify Recipe Management: Provide a digital solution to easily store, organize, and search for recipes.
2. Enhance Cooking Skills: Help users learn new cooking techniques through available recipes and tutorials.

Benefits and Functions of the Application
Benefits for Users:
● Time Efficiency: Quick recipe searches based on ingredients available at home
● Effective Organization: Storing recipes in structured, easily accessible categories
● Culinary Inspiration: Discovering new cooking ideas from other users in the community
● Skill Development: Enhancing cooking abilities through recipes with clear instructions
Key Functions:
● Personal Recipe Storage: Saving favorite recipes and original culinary creations
● Search and Filtering: Finding recipes based on specific ingredients
● Favorites and Bookmarks: Saving preferred recipes for quick access
